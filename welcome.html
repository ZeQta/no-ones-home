🎯 Controls

  • WASD - Move around
  • Mouse - Look around
  • Click Screen - Lock pointer (required!)
  • ESC - Unlock pointer / Pause
  • E - Interact (full game only)
  • P - Phone (full game only)
⚠️ Important:
• You MUST click on the screen to lock your mouse pointer
• Without pointer lock, controls won't work
• Use Chrome or Firefox for best compatibility
• Headphones recommended for full experience
